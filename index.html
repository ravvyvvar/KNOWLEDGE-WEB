How to read the link colours

Lineage — historical descent or documented influence: one tradition, teacher or text genuinely grew out of another.

Teaching — a figure, text or practice that carries an idea: the Buddha and nirvana, the Torah and Judaism.

Parallel — dashed. Distant cultures reaching a similar idea independently: flood myths, dying-and-rising gods, golden rules.

Fusion — syncretism: two streams deliberately blended, as in Sikhism, Theosophy, Wicca or Zen.

About the sources. Each node and each connection lists its own research links in its detail view — chiefly Encyclopaedia Britannica, the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, the Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Wikipedia, and primary texts at Sacred-Texts and Project Gutenberg. “Parallel” links are comparative observations made by scholars of religion, not claims that one culture copied another. Traditions are described as their adherents and historians present them; inclusion is not endorsement of any teaching.
